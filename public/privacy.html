Privacy policy

Agave · last updated November 23, 2026

Agave is a recipe costing and inventory tool for bars, restaurants and breweries. This page says what it keeps, why, and who else touches it. Short version: your data is used to run Agave for you, nothing else. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

What Agave keeps

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored scrambled; nobody can read it).
  • Your place's data: what you and your team put in: recipes, products, prices, vendors, invoices, counts, waste, menus, and recipe photos.
  • Your team: the email addresses and roles of the people in your place.
  • Sales, if you connect Square: item names, quantities and totals from your orders, used to work out what was poured. Agave doesn't ask Square for customer details.
  • Scan counts: how many invoice and recipe pages your place has scanned, to keep within limits.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores Agave's data and handles sign-in.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the invoice and recipe photos you scan and sends back the text. The photos aren't kept by Agave after they're read.
  • Square, only if you connect it, to send your sales to Agave.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

Each of these only gets what it needs to do its part.

On your device

Agave keeps a copy of your place's data on your phone or computer so it works without a connection, plus settings like dark mode and sounds. Signing out clears your place's copy.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app: tap your profile, then Account, then Delete my account. That deletes your sign-in, and any place you're the only owner of, with all its data and photos. Places you share with another owner stay for them. Want something removed without deleting your account? Email us.

Children

Agave is a business tool for adults and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date at the top.
